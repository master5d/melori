Third-party component: eSpeak NG
================================

This directory bundles eSpeak NG version 1.52.0:

  - espeak-ng.exe        (command-line phonemizer executable)
  - libespeak-ng.dll     (used by espeak-ng.exe)
  - espeak-ng-data/       (phoneme tables + English/Russian dictionaries)
